# WhatsUpDoc – Quick guide for doctors

**WhatsUpDoc** helps you manage appointments and messages with patients entirely through WhatsApp. No extra app to install. You and your patients keep using WhatsApp as usual; the bot handles scheduling, reminders, and safe replies.

---

## What you need to do once

1. **Save the clinic’s WhatsUpDoc number** in your WhatsApp (you’ll get this number from your clinic admin).
2. **Send one join message** if you’re using a sandbox/test number (your admin will give you the exact phrase, e.g. “join xxx-yyy”).
3. **You’re set.** From then on, just text that number like a normal WhatsApp chat.

---

## What you can do by texting the bot

Send normal messages in English (or your preferred language). Examples:

### Block your calendar
- *"Block tomorrow 10-1 surgery"*
- *"Block tomorrow 2 pm to 4 pm"*

### Set leave / vacation
- *"Vacation 5 June to 12 June"*
- *"Leave from 10th to 15th March"*

The bot will block those dates so patients don’t get those slots.

### Schedule a patient (by name)
- *"Schedule Ramesh Friday 5 pm"*
- *"Book Neha tomorrow 10 am"*

(Patient must already be in the system from having messaged the clinic number; otherwise the clinic may need to add them.)

### Cancel a patient’s appointment
- *"Cancel Neha tomorrow"*
- *"Cancel Ramesh"*

### Reschedule
- *"Move all tomorrow patients to next Monday"*  
  (Bulk reschedule – use when you need to shift a full day.)

### See your day
- *"Show appointments tomorrow"*
- *"List appointments for Friday"*

### Auto-reply when you’re away
- *"Auto-reply that I am on leave till Monday"*

The bot can be set to tell patients you’re away and when you’re back (your admin may need to enable this).

---

## What patients see

- **First time:** They get a short consent message. After they reply “Yes” or “Haan”, they can book, cancel, or ask for slots.
- **Booking:** They say things like *“Kal appointment mil sakti hai?”* or *“Can I come tomorrow?”*  
  The bot offers slots; they reply with the slot number (e.g. *“1”*) to confirm.
- **Language:** The bot can work in Hindi, English, and other Indian languages. Patients get replies in their language when possible.

---

## Important: the bot does NOT give medical advice

- It **does not** diagnose, prescribe, or give treatment advice.
- If a patient writes about pain, swelling, bleeding, medicines, or emergencies, the bot replies:  
  **“This automated assistant cannot provide medical advice. Your message has been forwarded to the clinic.”**  
  and forwards the message to the clinic so you or your staff can respond.

---

## Tips

- Use **clear dates and times** (e.g. “tomorrow 10 am”, “5 June to 12 June”).
- For **vacation**, include both start and end dates.
- If a command doesn’t work, try rephrasing (e.g. “Block tomorrow 10 to 1” or “Schedule Ramesh for Friday 5 pm”).

- Changing clinic hours or settings
